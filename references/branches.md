# Branch contracts

Use only the section for the branch being generated. Do not import its material language into another branch.

## 1. `abstraction`

### Objective

Create a complete abstract artwork from the photograph's expressive center. Let force, interval, rhythm, direction, expression, mass, light, or structural tension carry the source; do not make recognizability the primary goal.

Recognizability is not a goal. If a source-specific cue survives, let it survive as residue within the abstract relation, not as a preserved object or silhouette.

### Method Brief

Resolve before prompting:

- expressive center and one dominant perceptual event;
- at least one perceptual or relational source fact, plus zero to three nonliteral residues;
- what descriptive reality must disappear;
- one primary mark grammar and, only if necessary, one supporting grammar;
- attention geometry: dominant weight, quiet field, intervals, and movement;
- color action: what relationship it carries and where it acts.

### Artistic direction

- Write one internal proposition from the photograph's strongest expressive relationship.
- Choose one dominant perceptual event and one to three quieter echoes.
- Use a few large related forms with one dominant mark family and, only when useful, one supporting family.
- Let quiet space, scale, interval, and asymmetry carry as much meaning as the printed forms.
- Compress or shift the source palette to strengthen the proposition. Preserve color relationships when meaningful, not every original hue.

The expressive center is source-dependent. In a portrait it may be attitude, gaze, stance, or interpersonal distance; in an object-led image it may be weight, repetition, contact, or contrast; in a landscape it may be horizon, depth, weather, light, or spatial rhythm. These are readings, not fixed scene classes or visible templates.

When a person carries the source's meaning, preserve attitude or emotional pressure through direction, balance, interval, asymmetry, and visual weight. Do not rebuild anatomy, assign a symbol to each limb, or force the figure into a fixed body schema. Treat a small face, head, or hand as one indivisible cue when it must remain; otherwise let it disappear into the larger relation.

### Title policy

An internal working title may summarize the current image and test semantic coherence. It must be newly derived, optional, invisible by default, and unable to override the observed source relationships.

### Avoid

Photographic pixels, literal tracing, complete silhouettes, body diagrams, explanatory anatomy, generic geometric portraits, decorative symbols, excessive fragmentation, fixed palettes, fixed titles, and photo-plus-panel layouts.

## 2. `extend`

### Objective

Keep a truthful photographic anchor, then create a materially independent field from what the artwork chooses to omit, compress, interrupt, or continue. The new field must reframe or complete the scene rather than decorate its background.

The photographic anchor is factual material, not an image to repaint. The extension must become an independent voice rather than a disguised background treatment.

### Method Brief

Resolve before prompting:

- photographic anchor and the source facts protected inside it;
- source material to omit, compress, interrupt, or continue;
- one grammar for the independent material field;
- photo-to-field allocation and the compositional role of each side;
- one source-derived handoff between photography and the new field;
- color role, attachment, and path across or around the handoff, or an explicit decision to add no new color.

### Artistic direction

- Define one proposition for the encounter between photography and the new material field.
- Choose a photographic anchor by narrative and compositional value, not by tracing the primary subject. It may preserve a broad scene, cross through contextual space, or deliberately omit part of the apparent subject when the remaining relationship is stronger.
- Use secondary structures as raw material: a repeated rhythm may become a field, a shadow may become an interval, foliage may become a mass, and atmosphere may become an opening. Simplification serves the new composition; it is not background cleanup.
- Let the extension occupy enough visual territory to act as an independent voice. It may begin inside, beside, or beyond the original scene.
- Choose a source-responsive arrangement such as a broad field, open aperture, directional fragment, interrupted plane, or layered passage. Treat these as possibilities, not templates.

### Independent material language

- Let the handoff use direct contact or a clean cut, material diffusion, a source form or path continuing across the boundary, or another source-derived solution. A visible edge effect is unnecessary when the relationship is already clear.
- Derive the extension grammar from a source rhythm, material, shadow, atmosphere, gap, or direction rather than from a generic collage vocabulary.
- Keep the retained photography truthful. Do not repaint or regenerate it merely to unify the surface.
- A torn edge is optional, never mandatory.
- Extend one source axis, path, shadow, rhythm, atmospheric field, or material behavior. Do not reproduce a reference poster's signature arrangement.
- Use accent color only when it crosses, connects, replaces, redirects, or counterbalances a real source structure.
- Added typography is absent unless the user explicitly requests it. Factual text already inside the truthful photographic anchor remains source material rather than a new design layer.

### Avoid

Reference-like vertical templates, obligatory cream paper, obligatory torn contours, generic orange trails, lower-right microtext by habit, fitted subject masks, simple background restyling, scrapbook decoration, fully regenerated polished scenes, and extensions that merely imitate or enlarge the photo.

## 3. `reduction`

### Objective

Use the photograph as semantic evidence only and create a complete original artwork with an independent material language and its own proposition. Preserve the smallest source-specific meaning while allowing the original composition and descriptive reality to fall away.

Source specificity is a goal here: preserve enough evidence to distinguish this scene from a generic icon even after descriptive reality is removed.

### Method Brief

Resolve before prompting:

- one essential source relationship and one to three source-specific anchors;
- descriptive reality and redundant objects to release;
- one decisive formal rewrite that changes the source rather than abbreviating it;
- one primary illustration grammar and any necessary quiet echo;
- tonal hierarchy, quiet space, and one intentionally unresolved relation;
- color action attached to a semantic or directional pressure point, or an explicit decision to add no accent.

### Artistic direction

- Preserve one essential source relationship and one to three anchors drawn from gesture, material behavior, spatial tension, or a distinctive cue.
- Remove logos, surface description, redundant background, realistic lighting, and photographic texture.
- Make one decisive formal rewrite do most of the work: exaggerate scale or interval, merge forms, redirect movement, turn material behavior into a field, or use omission and negative space as structure.
- Use one primary illustration grammar and, only when it deepens the proposition, one quieter echo.
- Keep enough articulation for the subject or event to remain specific, but do not reconstruct every object or anatomical part.
- Use an environmental response—such as pressure, wake, wind, shadow, rhythm, or light—only when it carries meaning. It is optional, not a required system.
- Leave one relation intentionally unresolved so the viewer can complete it.

### Tonal control

- Break a large dark mass with adjacent values, paper gaps, dry-ink erosion, or broken edges only when the mass becomes visually sealed.
- Keep small anchors stable and undivided.
- Let tonal contrast establish hierarchy rather than describe realistic volume.
- Attach any high-chroma accent to a semantic pressure point, focal relationship, or directional handoff.

### Avoid

Photo pixels, tracing, sealed black slabs, excessive anatomy, over-segmentation, empty iconography, literal full-scene illustration, decorative metaphors, and detail retained only to prove fidelity.
