# Prompt compiler

Turn the Scene Card and one completed Method Brief into a generation prompt. Write three compact paragraphs in the order below. Do not expose the field labels or hidden analysis to the image model; translate them into visible decisions.

Use one proposition, one primary visual grammar, and only the supporting decisions needed to make the artwork complete. Added titles and typography are absent unless the user explicitly requests them. Edge effects, paper aging, and high-chroma color are optional and absent unless requested or the current source and method give them a visible job. Factual text inside a truthful `extend` anchor may remain as source material; never invent typography as decoration or promotion.

## 1. Proposition and attention geometry

State:

- the supplied photograph's role for the selected method;
- the artistic proposition and its visible consequence;
- source-responsive orientation or ratio;
- dominant weight, focal encounter, eye movement, and quiet field;
- the source relationships and anchors that must remain perceptible.

Describe what the viewer will see, not why the analysis chose it.

When it clarifies the proposition, begin from one source-responsive spatial tendency: concentrated weight in a quiet field, directional passage along a source gesture or path, relational interval between two weights, or uneven echoes around a core. Invent another arrangement when the photograph requires it. These are starting points, not layout families; never force percentages, expose their labels, or reuse them as templates.

## 2. Method construction

Compile only the selected method:

### `abstraction`

Name the dominant perceptual event, at least one surviving perceptual relation, any nonliteral residue, the primary mark grammar, important intervals, palette transformation, and descriptive reality to remove. Build a complete abstract field rather than a disguised figure, object, or photo-panel composition.

### `extend`

Define the truthful photographic anchor, protected source facts, independent material field, omitted or compressed source material, photo-to-field allocation, and source-derived handoff. State how the extension changes the composition or meaning rather than merely treating the background.

### `reduction`

Define the essential source relationship, retained anchors, discard list, one decisive formal rewrite, illustration grammar, tonal hierarchy, and one intentionally unresolved relation. Preserve enough source specificity to avoid a generic icon without reconstructing the original scene.

For every method, make retain, merge, release, translate, and leave-blank decisions visible through concrete forms, intervals, materials, or omissions. Do not paste the decision words themselves into the prompt.

## 3. Color, reproduction, and hard boundary

Resolve a compact Color Note:

- **source relation:** resonance, compression, harmony, or counterpoint;
- **role:** focal entry, counterweight, bridge, direction, atmosphere, or no added accent;
- **contrast:** intended value and chroma against neighboring forms;
- **attachment:** the source-derived form, interval, path, or pressure point it belongs to;
- **material and footprint:** printed block, dry ink, translucent field, paper form, or another fitting treatment and its approximate visual share;
- **removal test:** if removing the color changes nothing important, redesign or omit it.

Keep a small accent subordinate. Use a larger color passage only when color itself carries a principal spatial structure, and reduce competing detail accordingly. Adding no new accent is always valid.

Then state the reproduction behavior, material finish, output exclusions, and one method boundary verbatim:

- **`abstraction`:** `Use the supplied photograph only as perceptual reference. Do not retain, trace, embed, collage, or imitate photographic pixels, photorealistic regions, complete source silhouettes, or explanatory body diagrams.`
- **`extend`:** `Preserve the selected photographic anchor as truthful source material. Do not redraw, repaint, regenerate, filter, or cosmetically restyle it; make the non-photographic field materially independent and source-derived.`
- **`reduction`:** `Use the supplied photograph only as semantic and visual evidence. Do not retain, trace, embed, collage, or imitate photographic pixels or photorealistic regions; construct every visible form anew.`

End with only constraints relevant to the current artwork. Always exclude newly generated logos, watermarks, credits, fabricated metadata, promotional text, detached decoration, and recognizable material or layout formulas borrowed from another method or reference. Existing marks inside a truthful `extend` anchor may remain as source pixels rather than a new design layer.

## Compile gate

Generate only after confirming:

- the selected method and its photographic-material rule are explicit;
- the Method Brief has no unresolved required field;
- one proposition, hierarchy, quiet field, and primary grammar are visible;
- every added element serves a source relationship or a necessary compositional function;
- color has a stated role and attachment, or is intentionally absent;
- the prompt describes one finished artwork rather than an analysis report or a menu of alternatives.
